Social Welfare Initiatives

Discover the verified programs through which Lakshya Welfare Society has served families, young learners, and village residents in Chandoi, Pilibhit since 2011.

Core Program

Community Welfare & Social Assistance

Grassroots social assistance rooted in the rural realities of Chandoi and surrounding hamlets.

Our community welfare initiative operates with a simple, respectful principle: assist people with dignity. Through direct surveys and communication with village elders, Lakshya Welfare Society identifies households experiencing acute distress—such as elderly citizens without care, families struggling through sudden medical emergencies, or vulnerable daily-wage workers.

Key Activities

  • Direct household welfare assessments in rural clusters of Chandoi
  • Emergency first-aid demonstrations and worker health literacy sessions
  • Voluntary community meetings to resolve neighborhood social challenges

Education

Education & Children's Learning Support

Ensuring that no child drops out of school due to lack of essential learning materials.

In many rural families in Pilibhit, the cost of basic stationery, notebooks, and learning supplies can place a burden on modest household budgets. Lakshya Welfare Society organizes periodic school supply drives, providing high-quality notebooks, pencils, school bags, and primary study aids to children.

In addition to material distribution, volunteers talk with parents to encourage consistent attendance, especially advocating for first-generation learners and girls.

Focus Areas

  • Distribution of notebooks, writing supplies, and learning aids
  • Counselling parents on the importance of uninterrupted primary education
  • Creating an encouraging, positive environment for young students

Health & Wellness

Rural Health Awareness & Medical Camps

Preventive healthcare awareness, health screenings, and medical advice for rural families.

Founded in 2011 by Dr. Riaz Ahmad, healthcare accessibility is central to the mission of Lakshya Welfare Society. Rural communities frequently experience preventable health ailments due to delayed diagnosis or lack of awareness regarding seasonal diseases, safe drinking water, and hygiene.

Our health camps bring qualified medical guidance right into village settlements. Residents receive basic vital check-ups, blood pressure monitoring, dietary counselling, and timely referrals to formal medical centers when specialized treatment is required.

Camp Components

  • Free general health check-ups and doctor consultations
  • Hygiene, sanitation, and clean drinking water awareness
  • Diagnostic tests (ECG, blood sugar, oxygen saturation)

Relief Aid

Essential Family Relief & Seasonal Support

Timely assistance during harsh winters and unforeseen household distress.

Winters in northern Uttar Pradesh can be severe for elderly citizens and families residing in modest rural homes. Lakshya Welfare Society organizes seasonal relief distributions, including warm blankets, winter clothing, and essential food items, ensuring community care reaches those most exposed to winter conditions.
