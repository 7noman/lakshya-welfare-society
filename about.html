About Lakshya Welfare Society

Founded in 2011, dedicated to localized community welfare, children’s learning encouragement, and public health awareness across rural Chandoi and Pilibhit district, Uttar Pradesh.

Guiding Purpose

"Every Life is Precious..."

Lakshya Welfare Society was founded in 2011 with an unwavering belief in human dignity and collective responsibility. Our motto, "Every life is precious...", shapes every community visit, health camp, and relief effort we undertake.

In rural northern India, communities often encounter barriers in accessing regular preventive healthcare, quality learning resources for young children, and rapid social assistance during seasonal or medical emergencies. Lakshya Welfare Society serves as a compassionate bridge—identifying localized gaps and responding through direct community engagement.

Direct Grassroots Aid

Meeting families where they live, understanding individual hardship, and delivering direct material assistance.

Health & Hygiene

Promoting preventive medical literacy, health check-ups, and maternal and child well-being in rural clusters.

Educational Upliftment

Equipping village school children with stationery and notebooks to build a lifelong foundation of learning.

Ethical Stewardship

Treating all community support with honesty, zero fabricated claims, and respectful care of public resources.

Leadership & Founder

Meet Dr. Riaz Ahmad

Founder & Chief Functionary

Dr. Riaz Ahmad

Dr. Riaz Ahmad is the founder and chief functionary of Lakshya Welfare Society, founded in 2011 in Chandoi, Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh. Motivated by the urgent social and healthcare needs of rural households, Dr. Riaz Ahmad organized community members to establish a permanent platform for welfare assistance.

Under his direction, the organization has coordinated village outreach meetings, free health screening consultations, and educational distribution drives. His approach emphasizes direct, personal involvement with community elders and local families rather than bureaucratic distance.

Regional Focus

Serving Uttar Pradesh

Understanding the local geography is essential to understanding the work of Lakshya Welfare Society.

Pilibhit is a northern district of Uttar Pradesh known for its lush agrarian plains, river basins, and rural settlements. In villages like Chandoi, agricultural livelihood forms the backbone of family life. During adverse weather, agricultural downturns, or medical emergencies, rural families face acute difficulties.

Lakshya Welfare Society is firmly grounded in this reality. Rather than operating as a remote office, the society’s volunteers live and work among the people they serve, ensuring that assistance reaches those who need it most without administrative delay.

Important Disambiguation:

Several non-profit entities in India share the name "Lakshya". This official website represents Lakshya Welfare Society located in Chandoi, Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh, founded in 2011 by Dr. Riaz Ahmad (Official Facebook: facebook.com/lwsociety). Please do not confuse this organization with similarly named organizations in other districts or states.

Public Accountability

Our Commitment to Transparency

We believe an NGO must earn the trust of its community and supporters every single day.

Factual Information Policy

  • No Fabricated Impact Counters: We do not publish made-up statistics such as "thousands of lives transformed" without audited records.
  • Real Activity Photographs: All imagery and activity reports reflect actual community meetings, distributions, and health camps organized in the region.
  • No Unverified Tax Claims: We do not advertise tax exemptions under Section 80G or foreign contribution licenses (FCRA) unless officially certified. Donors requesting receipts are guided to our direct contact channel.
  • Protected Beneficiary Dignity: We respect the privacy and dignity of all beneficiaries, particularly children and vulnerable community members.
