How Your Contribution is Put to Work

Lakshya Welfare Society is a grassroots social welfare organization founded in 2011. Contributions received from donors, patrons, and well-wishers are directed toward our verified rural programs:

Rural Health Screening & Medical Literacy

Health

Procuring basic medical screening equipment, first aid supplies, and coordinating volunteer doctor visits.

Children's Educational Kits & Schooling Support

Education

Purchasing notebooks, writing supplies, and educational resources for village school children.

Seasonal Family Relief & Winter Warmth

Relief

Supplying warm blankets and dry ration essentials to vulnerable rural families during difficult winters.

Trust & Integrity

Frequently Asked Questions on Donating

Clear, honest answers regarding donations, tax status, and payment verification.

Are donations eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G?

In accordance with ethical disclosure guidelines, Lakshya Welfare Society does not claim tax-deductible status under Section 80G until all relevant statutory certifications are formally verified and published. Please verify current registration details directly with the organization prior to claiming charitable tax deductions.

Does the organization accept foreign contributions (FCRA)?

No. Lakshya Welfare Society accepts contributions made in Indian Rupees (INR) from resident individuals and domestic organizations within India. We do not solicit or accept foreign funds under FCRA regulations.

Can I donate physical materials (books, school supplies, warm clothes)?

Yes! In-kind contributions of stationery, children's books, hygiene kits, and winter blankets are welcomed. Please contact the team in advance to arrange collection or delivery in Chandoi or Pilibhit city.

How do I confirm my donation was received?

After completing a UPI transfer or bank payment, kindly take a screenshot of the transaction showing the UTR / reference number and message our official Facebook page or contact form. Our volunteer team will confirm receipt.
