Working Together for a Stronger Community

Based in the rural village of Chandoi in Pilibhit district, Lakshya Welfare Society was founded in 2011 by Dr. Riaz Ahmad to address community needs through direct social assistance, child education, and public health awareness.

Founder & Chief Functionary

Dr. Riaz Ahmad

Dr. Riaz Ahmad founded Lakshya Welfare Society in 2011 to serve the people of Chandoi and surrounding rural areas in Uttar Pradesh. Guided by the principle that "Every life is precious...", the society focuses on providing genuine grassroots support, encouraging schooling for young children, organizing health consultations, and assisting households experiencing social or economic hardship.

Primary Program

Community Welfare & Social Assistance

Grassroots outreach aimed at supporting disadvantaged families, rural elders, and vulnerable village residents through direct assistance, seasonal aid, and community solidarity in Chandoi.

  • Direct household identification of families requiring emergency support
  • Seasonal relief distribution (warm clothing, essential provisions)
  • Community collaboration with village elders and local volunteers
Learn more about community welfare
Education

Children's Learning Support

Encouraging regular school attendance, supplying notebooks, textbooks, and stationery to first-generation learners from modest backgrounds in rural Pilibhit.

  • Distribution of basic learning and stationery supplies
  • Community advocacy for regular schooling of girls and boys
View educational initiatives
Health Outreach

Rural Health Awareness Camps

Promoting public health awareness, hygiene practices, and organizing rural health screening camps in collaboration with local medical practitioners.

  • General medical consultations and wellness screenings
  • Preventive guidance on seasonal health and clean drinking water
